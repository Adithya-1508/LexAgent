# Official UAE Legal Sources Corpus

This directory contains official source metadata, raw documents, and structured provision records for UAE Labour Law.

## Provenance Rules
1. **Authoritative Language**: Arabic (`ar`) is the sole legal source of truth under UAE Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, Article 66).
2. **Aligned Translations**: English (`en`) provisions are non-authoritative translations linked directly to their parent Arabic provision (`translation_of`).
3. **Coverage Status**: The initial MVP dataset is marked as `partial_mvp` covering critical employment articles (Articles 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 42, 43, 51, 52, 53, 66) and Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022.
