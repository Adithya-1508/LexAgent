# LexAgent Phase 5.2 — Specification (`spec.md`)

## 1. Objective & Scope

Expand LexAgent's statutory corpus from the Phase 5.1 `partial_mvp` state (13 of 74 articles of FDL 33/2021 and 1 article of Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022, backed by 4 unattested derived `.txt` files) into a provenance-verified, bilingual, amendment-aware corpus with fail-closed retrieval, partial-release quarantine support, and version-scoped citation verification.

### In Scope (Phase 5.2)
- **Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021** (full 74-article target, AR authoritative + official EN).
- **Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022** (Executive Regulation, full 39-article expansion target, AR authoritative + official EN).
- **Amending Federal Decree-Laws:**
  - Federal Decree-Law No. 14 of 2022 (amending Art. 8(3)).
  - Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2023 (amending Art. 54, effective 01 Jan 2024: 15 working days appeal to Court of Appeal, court decides within 15 working days, 1-yr limitation).
  - Federal Decree-Law No. 9 of 2024 (amending Arts. 54 and 60, effective 31 Aug 2024 per Official Gazette No. 780 after 30-day commencement formula under Art. 4: appeal venue shifted to Court of First Instance within 15 working days, 2-yr limitation from termination, pending-case transition rule under Art. 2).
- Historical version reconstruction for amended provisions (Arts. 8(3), 54, 60) with explicit uncertainty windows where historical Gazette dates require boundary modeling.
- Mandatory applicability caveat on Article 51 referencing Cabinet Resolution No. 96 of 2023 (Alternative End-of-Service Savings Scheme), with claim-key `gratuity_entitlement_service_threshold` binding strictly to Article 51(2) (Foreign Worker severance).

### Explicitly Out of Scope (Boundary Registry / Phase 5.3+)
- Full ingestion of **Cabinet Resolution No. 96 of 2023** (Voluntary Alternative EOSB Savings Scheme) — deferred to Phase 5.3 per owner decision.
- **Cabinet Resolution No. 33 of 2022** (Work Injuries Tables) and **Cabinet Resolution No. 18 of 2022** (Establishment Classification) — tracked in Boundary Registry for Phase 5.3+.
- **Ministerial Resolution No. 340 of 2026** (WPS rules effective 01 Jun 2026) — tracked in Boundary Registry so LexAgent does not assert stale WPS grace periods.
- **Excluded Jurisdictions:** DIFC Employment Law, ADGM Employment Regulations, Domestic Workers (FDL 9/2022), Federal Public-Sector HR Law.

## 2. User & System Scenarios

- **S-01 (Undated / Current-Law Query with Positive Topic Certification & Mandatory Disclosure):** A user asks about the limitation period for labour claims without specifying a date. The engine first verifies that Article 54 is positively certified in `BoundaryRegistry.verified_topics` and has no uningested post-cutoff amendments. It then binds to `effective_as_of_max` (`2024-08-31`), selects the post-FDL 9/2024 version of Article 54 (2 years from termination), verifies against an approved claim-key entry, and returns the grounded answer accompanied by the mandatory `TEMPORAL_CUTOFF_DISCLOSURE`. The disclosure is propagated through both `GroundedLegalAnswer` and `LegalResponse`.
- **S-02 (Historical Dispute Query):** A user asks about MoHRE's claim-resolution authority `as_of=2024-03-15`. LexAgent resolves the FDL 20/2023 version of Article 54 (effective 01 Jan 2024 until superseded by FDL 9/2024 on 31 Aug 2024), advising of the Court of Appeal venue and attaching `HISTORICAL_ANSWER_CAVEAT` without silently serving the later Court-of-First-Instance amendment.
- **S-03 (Temporal Uncertainty Window & Future Date Rejection):** A user queries Article 8(3) on a date falling inside a simulated or historical unresolved Gazette publication window (or queries a future date > `2024-08-31`). LexAgent fails closed with `abstention_reason="temporal_uncertainty"`.
- **S-04 (Caveated Provision Fail-Closed & Clause-Specific Gratuity Binding):** A user queries end-of-service gratuity. LexAgent identifies Article 51 Clause 2 (`FDL_33_2021_ART_51_02`, which carries `STATUTORY_INTERACTION_CR_96_2023`). Under default `include_caveated=False`, ordinary retrieval fails closed. Under explicit opt-in (`include_caveated=True` + mandatory disclosure policy), LexAgent serves Article 51(2) and `CitationVerifier` enforces that the CR 96/2023 applicability caveat is rendered in the final response.
- **S-05 (Partial Corpus Release):** Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022 has verified provisions (`VERIFIED_PUBLISHABLE`) and remaining unextracted articles (`DocumentCompleteness.PARTIAL_VERIFIED`). Queries matching verified articles succeed; queries targeting missing uningested articles fail closed and abstain (`abstention_reason="incomplete_statutory_coverage"`).
- **S-06 (Corrupted PDF Quarantine):** An amending decree PDF exhibits Arabic CMap glyph corruption. Extraction flags `cmap_corruption_detected=True`, quarantining affected provisions as `BLOCKED_LEGAL_TOPIC` until human extraction verification (`extraction_verified`) is recorded.
- **S-07 (Stale / Post-Cutoff Amendment or Incomplete Registry Abstention):** A user submits an undated query regarding wage protection rules or an uncertified topic. The engine identifies that Ministerial Resolution No. 340 of 2026 is registered in `BoundaryRegistry.post_cutoff_amendments` (or the topic is missing from `verified_topics`). Rather than asserting outdated rules, the engine fails closed with `abstention_reason="stale_or_incomplete_amendment_coverage"` or `abstention_reason="incomplete_amendment_verification"`.
- **S-08 (Pending-Case Transition Advice under FDL 9/2024 Art. 2):** A user queries dispute resolution procedures for a qualifying dispute pending before the Court of Appeal under Article 54(3) on 31 August 2024. LexAgent explains the statutory transfer *ex officio* to the Court of First Instance without judicial fees per Article 2 of FDL 9/2024, explicitly highlighting the statutory exceptions for cases already decided or reserved for judgment.
- **S-09 (180-Day Freshness Horizon Clock Guardrail):** The wall-clock execution date exceeds `system_cutoff` (`2024-08-31`) by more than 180 days. All current-law / undated queries fail closed with `abstention_reason="corpus_verification_expired"`. A warning alone is prohibited from authorizing a current-law answer. Substantive answers are permitted only for callers who explicitly specify a historical `as_of` date (`as_of <= 2024-08-31`) with verified topic coverage.
- **S-10 (Provision Present but Historical Coverage Uncertified):** A user submits an explicit historical query (`as_of = 2022-06-01` <= `system_cutoff`). A candidate `LegalProvision` exists in the corpus covering that date interval, but `BoundaryRegistry.verified_topics` lacks affirmative verified topic coverage for that statutory topic at that historical date (coverage is unlisted, `UNKNOWN`, or `INCOMPLETE`). The engine refuses to generate a substantive answer based solely on interval matching; it fails closed and abstains with `abstention_reason="unverified_historical_topic_coverage"`.
- **S-11 (Qualifying Pending Lawsuit Transferred to First Instance):** A labour dispute under Article 54(3) (challenge to MoHRE summary decision) was instituted prior to 31 August 2024, was pending before the Court of Appeal on 31 August 2024, was not decided, and was not reserved for judgment. LexAgent identifies that Branch 10 of FDL 9/2024 Article 2 applies: the lawsuit is transferred *ex officio* without judicial fees to the Court of First Instance, which assumes trial jurisdiction, and attaches `PENDING_CASE_TRANSITION_DISCLOSURE`.
- **S-12 (Decided Case Exception under Art. 2):** A labour dispute under Article 54(3) was decided by the Court of Appeal prior to or as of 31 August 2024. LexAgent identifies that Branch 8 (statutory decision exception under Article 2) applies: the case is not transferred and remains under the finality of the Court of Appeal judgment.
- **S-13 (Reserved-for-Judgment Case Exception under Art. 2):** A labour dispute under Article 54(3) was pending before the Court of Appeal on 31 August 2024 and was formally reserved for judgment (*محجوزة للحكم*). LexAgent identifies that Branch 9 (statutory exception under Article 2) applies: the matter is excluded from transfer and jurisdiction remains with the Court of Appeal to render judgment.
- **S-14 (Article 54(4) Unresolved Referral Inapplicability under Art. 2):** A dispute involves an unresolved labour complaint referred by MoHRE to court under Article 54(4), rather than a challenge to a summary decision under Article 54(3). LexAgent identifies that Branch 5 applies: the transitional transfer rule of Article 2 applies strictly to Article 54(3) challenges and does not apply to Article 54(4) referrals; it does not infer court venue or transfer from Article 2, but evaluates ordinary referral procedural rules or abstains.
- **S-15 (Historical Dispute Query Pre-Commencement):** A user asks about dispute appeal procedures as of `as_of = 2024-05-01` (prior to FDL 9/2024 commencement). LexAgent applies Branch 3: the governing statutory version is FDL 20/2023 (appeals against summary decisions are filed before the Court of Appeal within 15 working days, the court decides within 15 working days, and a 1-year limitation applies); the later Article 2 transfer rule does not execute, and `HISTORICAL_ANSWER_CAVEAT` is attached.
- **S-16 (Ambiguous Procedural Facts Abstention):** A user query indicates a dispute was filed in June 2024, but omits essential procedural facts (such as whether it was an Art. 54(3) challenge or Art. 54(4) referral, whether it was pending, decided, or reserved for judgment as of 31 August 2024). LexAgent applies Branch 7: the engine refuses to guess trial vs. appellate venue by filing date alone; it fails closed with `abstention_reason="missing_procedural_facts"` or prompts for clarification.
- **S-17 (Post-Commencement Filing Venue):** A labour dispute challenging a MoHRE summary decision under Article 54(3) is instituted after 31 August 2024. LexAgent applies Branch 11: Article 2 pending-case transfer does not apply (because the lawsuit was not pending before the Court of Appeal at commencement); the lawsuit is filed directly before the competent Court of First Instance within 15 working days under Article 54(1) as amended by FDL 9/2024.
- **S-18 (Non-Pending Lawsuit at Commencement):** A labour dispute under Article 54(3) was filed before the Court of Appeal prior to 31 August 2024 but was dismissed, withdrawn, or concluded before commencement (and was neither pending nor reserved for judgment on 31 August 2024). LexAgent applies Branch 11: Article 2 transfer does not apply; ordinary procedural rules apply.

## 3. Requirements & Acceptance Criteria

| Req ID | Requirement | Acceptance Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **REQ-01** | Five-dimension source provenance & redistribution compliance | 100% of new snapshots record `authenticity_tier`, SHA-256, `redistribution_clearance`, and (for manual files) a validated `ManualAttestation`. Synthetic fixtures strictly marked `SYNTHETIC_TEST_ONLY` with zero publication validity claims. |
| **REQ-02** | HTML-first deterministic extraction & dual-track Arabic normalization | Deterministic re-run hash equality = 100%; `text` remains verbatim while `normalized_text` powers search; CMap corruption auto-detected. |
| **REQ-03** | Effective-date versioning & amendment lineage | Closed inclusive `[effective_from, effective_until]` windows with `AmendmentEdge` links for Arts. 8(3), 54, and 60; explicit support for repeal, gaps, and uncertainty windows. |
| **REQ-04** | Multi-version indexing, affirmative coverage & temporal safety | `RetrievalIndex` supports multiple versions per `logical_provision_id`; affirmative topic certification in `BoundaryRegistry.verified_topics` strictly enforced for both current-law (with 180-day freshness clock failing closed) and historical queries (requiring positive verified historical coverage at `as_of`; interval match alone rejected); undated queries bind to `2024-08-31` with mandatory `TEMPORAL_CUTOFF_DISCLOSURE`; pending-case transition (FDL 9/2024 Art. 2) evaluated via 11-branch mutually exclusive ordered decision logic across scenarios S-11 to S-18; 100% pass on boundary tests. |
| **REQ-05** | Separated document completeness vs. provision eligibility | `LegalDocument.completeness` is decoupled from `LegalProvision.publication_state`; partially ingested documents serve independently verified provisions while quarantining defects. |
| **REQ-06** | Fail-closed defaults for caveated & legacy records | `RetrievalFilters.include_caveated=False` and `allow_legacy_unverified=False` by default; legacy provisions load as `LEGACY_UNVERIFIED` and never auto-promote; legacy opt-ins strictly confined to compat tests. |
| **REQ-07** | Separated bilingual validation & same-version alignment | `translation_provenance`, `structural_alignment_status`, `numeric_consistency_status`, and `semantic_equivalence_status` stored separately; cross-version alignment rejected; exact alignment strictly requires non-null `en_provision_id`; explicit `unavailable_official` EN permitted with caveat. |
| **REQ-08** | Universal evidence provenance gate, claim-key registry & clause binding | `claim_key_registry.json` replaces hardcoded `_ALIGNMENT_CLAIM_KEYS`; new provisions have 0 authorized claims until an approved entry is reviewed and checksummed; `gratuity_entitlement_service_threshold` strictly bound to Article 51(2); universal evidence provenance and eligibility gate enforced across `LegalSynthesisEngine.synthesize_from_evidence()`, retrieval-driven `synthesize()`, and `synthesis_node` in `agent/nodes.py`; strict filter defaults apply when omitted; claim IDs derive exclusively from verified evidence; dual-path acceptance tests verify parity across `GroundedLegalAnswer` and `LegalResponse`. |
| **REQ-09** | Unified release & quarantine validator (`RV-01`–`RV-11`) | `ingestion.py` and `evaluation/validate.py` share one validator engine; corruption or logical contradictions block release; entity defects quarantine cleanly. |
| **REQ-10** | Backward compatibility & contract tests | Phase 5.1 JSON fixtures validate without error; `core-minimal` CI job passes with `pydantic`-only core; explicit contract test suite verifies all deliberate behavior deltas. |
| **REQ-11** | Adversarial legal safety & multi-stage abstention | 100% deterministic invariant pass rate on DIFC/ADGM traps, non-existent articles, temporal boundaries, quarantined topics, pending-case transitions, unverified historical coverage, and undisclosed caveats across both `GroundedLegalAnswer` and `LegalResponse`. |
| **REQ-12** | Reproducible offline CI & versioned release | `release_manifest.json` reproduces identically offline in GitHub Actions using `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`; all 11 CI jobs pass. |
