## 1. Governance & Categorization Protocol

In accordance with strict safety engineering discipline, all findings and technical items are categorized into three unambiguous statuses:
- **`VERIFIED`**: Proven by repository source code, passing automated tests, or primary official government documents. Requires no further architectural debate.
- **`PROPOSED`**: Architecturally specified and scheduled for subsequent atomic PRs, but awaiting implementation and runtime verification in subsequent milestones.
- **`BLOCKED`**: Hard constraints, external dependencies, or security boundaries that prevent immediate automation or require explicit human intervention.
