## 1. Statutory Scope & Legal Integrity Principles

This document establishes the verified legal baseline for Phase 5.2 of LexAgent, auditing all primary statutory instruments, official gazette numbers, issuance and commencement dates, amending relationships, linguistic authorities, and access limitations.

Secondary sources (commercial HR portals, law firm summaries, and blogs) were systematically reviewed and **rejected** due to recurring substantive errors. All facts in this report are verified exclusively against official UAE government portals (`uaelegislation.gov.ae`, `mohre.gov.ae`) and the UAE Federal Official Gazette (*Al-Jarida Al-Rasmiyya*).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Honest Epistemological Rule:** Inaccessible primary evidence (such as upstream government PDF downloads gated behind anti-bot WAFs in automated CI environments) is **never** labeled as "independently verified". Unretrieved upstream artifacts and pending historical isolations are preserved as explicit open issues.

## 4. Technical Defect Analysis: Arabic PDF CMap Font Corruption

Official modification PDFs 1297 and 1760 embed subsetted fonts with corrupted ToUnicode CMap mapping tables.
- Standard extractors (`pdftotext`/PyMuPDF) emit presentation-form glyphs (e.g. `\ufe8e`) or replacement characters (`\ufffd`).
- **Architectural Remedy (`ADR-002`):** Phase 5.2 establishes **HTML-first deterministic extraction** from `uaelegislation.gov.ae`. PDF snapshots are preserved strictly as archival references (`source_role: "archival_reference"`). Any PDF-derived provision exhibiting CMap corruption is quarantined as `BLOCKED_LEGAL_TOPIC` until human extraction verification is recorded.
