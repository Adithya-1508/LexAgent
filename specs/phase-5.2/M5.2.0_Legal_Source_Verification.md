## 1. Statutory Scope & Mandate

This document establishes the verified legal baseline for Phase 5.2 of LexAgent, auditing all primary statutory instruments, official gazette numbers, issuance and commencement dates, amending relationships, and linguistic authorities.

Secondary sources (commercial HR portals, law firm summaries, and blogs) were systematically reviewed and **rejected** due to verified inaccuracies (such as misrepresenting the effective dates of amendments or claiming a 3-year employment contract maximum still applies). All facts in this report are verified exclusively against the official UAE legislation portal (`uaelegislation.gov.ae`), the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (`mohre.gov.ae`), and the UAE Federal Official Gazette (*Al-Jarida Al-Rasmiyya*).

### 3.1 Federal Decree-Law No. (14) of 2022
- **Issuance Date:** 15 September 2022 (19 Safar 1444 AH)
- **Official Gazette:** Issue No. 736, dated 28 September 2022

- **Issuance Date:** 13 September 2023 (27 Safar 1445 AH)

- **Issuance Date:** 29 July 2024 (23 Muharram 1446 AH)

- **Commencement Date:** **31 August 2024** (one month following publication in the Official Gazette, per Article 3)

## 4. Technical Defect Analysis: Arabic PDF CMap Corruption

Official UAE amendment PDFs (Modifications 1297 and 1760 on `uaelegislation.gov.ae`) embed subsetted Arabic fonts with incomplete or broken ToUnicode CMap tables. As a result:
- Standard deterministic extractors (`pdftotext`, PyMuPDF) emit presentation-form glyphs (e.g. `\ufe8e`, `\ufeaa`), unjoined letters, or Unicode replacement characters (`\ufffd`).
- For example, *الإمارات* is frequently extracted as `اإلمارات` or `امارات`.

### Architectural Mitigation: HTML-First Extraction (`ADR-002`)
The official portal (`uaelegislation.gov.ae`) renders the full statutory text in clean, logical-order UTF-8 HTML. Phase 5.2 adopts **HTML-first deterministic extraction** for all primary statutory texts. PDF snapshots are retained strictly as archival corroboration records (`source_role: "archival_reference"`). Any provision derived from a PDF exhibiting CMap corruption is automatically quarantined as `BLOCKED_LEGAL_TOPIC` until human verification (`extraction_verified`) is recorded.
