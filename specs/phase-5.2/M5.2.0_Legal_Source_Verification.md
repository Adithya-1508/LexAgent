## 1. Statutory Scope & Legal Integrity Principles

This document establishes the verified legal baseline for Phase 5.2 of LexAgent, auditing all primary statutory instruments, official gazette numbers, enactment and commencement dates, amending relationships, linguistic authorities, and access limitations.

Normative statutory claims rely on primary UAE sources. Secondary indexes and commentary are cited only to document conflicting or unverified publication and commencement claims.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Honest Epistemological Rule:** Inaccessible primary evidence (such as upstream government PDF downloads gated behind anti-bot WAFs in automated CI environments) is **never** labeled as "independently verified". Unretrieved upstream artifacts and pending historical isolations are preserved as explicit open issues with fail-closed downstream abstention requirements. Never convert an unverified source into a verified claim. English translations can **never** override authoritative Arabic text.

## 3. In-Depth Statutory Analysis of Challenged Legal Invariants

### 3.1 Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2023 (Dispute Jurisdiction & Deadlines)
- **Official Title:** Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2023 Amending Certain Provisions of Federal Decree-Law No. (33) of 2021 Regarding the Regulation of Employment Relationships
- **Enactment Date:** 13 September 2023 (27 Safar 1445 AH; primary Arabic text *27 صفر 1445 هـ*)
- **Official Gazette:** Exact issue and publication metadata have not been independently verified from the Gazette. A [secondary index](https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/uae/federal-decree/15-09-2023/no-20?id=2098749&type=1) lists Issue 759 (Supplement), 15 September 2023, conflicting with the earlier 28 September claim. The decree states the commencement date independently.
- **Commencement Date:** 1 January 2024 (per Article 2: *يُنشر هذا المرسوم بقانون في الجريدة الرسمية، ويُعمل به اعتباراً من 1 يناير 2024*)
- **Substantive Invariants:**
  1. **Summary Jurisdiction:** Empowered the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE) to issue final summary decisions on disputes where the total claim does not exceed AED 50,000, or disputes involving non-compliance with prior amicable settlement agreements.
  2. **Appeal Period & Working-Day Counting:** An aggrieved party may challenge the Ministry's summary decision by filing a lawsuit within **15 working days** (*15 يوم عمل* / *خمسة عشر يوم عمل*) from the date of notification. The court fixes a hearing within 3 working days and delivers a final judgment within 15 working days from filing. Working days exclude official public holidays and weekly rest days. Counting calendar days or 14 days constitutes a legal error.
  3. **Appeal Venue:** Under FDL 20/2023, the appeal was instituted directly before the **Court of Appeal** (*محكمة الاستئناف*).
  4. **Limitation Period:** Claims could not be heard if instituted more than **1 year from the date on which the right fell due** (*انقضاء سنة من تاريخ استحقاق الحق*).
  5. **Official English Copy & MoHRE Translation Provenance:** The UAE government did not release an official standalone English translation for FDL 20/2023 on the federal legislation portal (`uaelegislation.gov.ae`). While the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation publishes English summaries or translations on its administrative portal (`mohre.gov.ae`), these are strictly informational convenience translations and carry zero authoritative legal status. Under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 Article 66(2), the Arabic text is the sole legally approved reference. In LexAgent, this provision is assigned `translation_provenance="unavailable_official"` and attaches caveat `OFFICIAL_EN_UNAVAILABLE`.

### 3.2 Federal Decree-Law No. (9) of 2024 (Procedural Shift, Transition & Penalties)
- **Official Title:** Federal Decree-Law No. (9) of 2024 Amending Certain Provisions of Federal Decree-Law No. (33) of 2021 Regarding the Regulation of Employment Relationships
- **Enactment Date:** 29 July 2024 (23 Muharram 1446 AH)
- **Official Gazette:** A [secondary Gazette index](https://app.reallaw.ai/en/legal-table/doc/government-of-the-united-arab-emirates-official-gazette-issue-no-780-annex-639) lists No. 780 (Annex), 31 July 2024. The primary Gazette publication record has not been independently verified.
- **Commencement Formula and Evidence Status:** Article 4 prescribes entry into force after 30 days from publication. Secondary accounts report both [30 August](https://www.bdolegal.ae/en-gb/insights/private-sector-employment) and [31 August 2024](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cm0mihiiy008uvc5khrvmjvdz/key-amendments-to-the-uae-labour-law-federal-decree-law-no-9-of-2024). The exact date remains unresolved pending authoritative publication evidence and confirmation of the date calculation. Treat `[2024-08-30, 2024-08-31]` as an inclusive uncertainty window. The `2024-08-31` planning cutoff is an upper evaluation limit. Abstain with `temporal_uncertainty` within the window or whenever a definitive venue or transfer decision depends on unresolved commencement.
- **Article-by-Article Structure:**
  - **Article 1 (Substantive Amendments):**
    - **Venue Shift for Summary Appeals:** Replaces Article 54 so that appeals against MoHRE summary decisions are filed before the **competent Court of First Instance** (*المحكمة الابتدائية المختصة*), rather than the Court of Appeal. The appeal window is **15 working days** (*15 يوم عمل*) from notification.
    - **Limitation Period Extension:** Replaces the 1-year rule with a rule barring claims if **2 years have elapsed from the date of termination of the employment relationship** (*إذا انقضت سنتان على انتهاء علاقة العمل*).
    - **Amended Penalties (Article 60):** Enacts severe penalties (fines between AED 100,000 and AED 1,000,000) for employing workers without authorization, bringing workers to the state and leaving them without employment, closing an establishment without settling workers' rights, or fictitious employment (fake Emiratisation).
  - **Article 2 (Pending-Case Transition & Strict Exceptions):**
    - At verified commencement, the Court of Appeal transfers pending Article 54(3) challenges to the competent Court of First Instance as they stand, without judicial fees. Article 54(3) covers challenges to decisions made under Article 54(2), including noncompliance with a previous amicable settlement decision regardless of claim value. Article 54(4) referrals are outside the transfer rule.
    - **Statutory Exceptions:** A judgment already delivered stands. A case reserved for pronouncement of judgment stays with the Court of Appeal. Article 2 excludes both from transfer.
  - **Article 3 (Repeals):** Repeals any provision contrary to or inconsistent with this Decree-Law (*يُلغى كل حكم يخالف أو يتعارض مع أحكام هذا المرسوم بقانون*).
  - **Article 4 (Publication & Entry into Force):** The published text specifies the 30-day formula. Apply the commencement evidence gate above before resolving version boundaries or selecting a court.

### 3.4 Article 51(1) vs Article 51(2): Gratuity Eligibility & Claim Binding
A critical statutory distinction exists between Clause 1 and Clause 2 of Article 51 of FDL 33/2021:
- **Article 51(1) (National Workers / *العامل المواطن*):**
  - Text: *يستحق العامل المواطن مكافأة نهاية الخدمة عند انتهاء خدمته وذلك وفقًا للتشريعات المنظمة للمعاشات والتأمينات الاجتماعية السارية في الدولة.*
  - Rule: Emirati national workers are covered by federal pension and social security schemes (managed by GPSSA and ADRPBF). They do not receive private statutory gratuity under Article 51(2).
- **Article 51(2) (Foreign Workers / *العامل الأجنبي*):**
  - Text: *يستحق العامل الأجنبي الذي يعمل وفق نمط العمل بالدوام الكامل، والذي أكمل سنة أو أكثر في الخدمة المستمرة مكافأة نهاية الخدمة عند انتهاء خدمته، تحسب وفق الأجر الأساسي...*
  - Rule: Expatriate employees working under a full-time pattern who complete 1 or more years of continuous service are entitled to severance calculated on basic wage (21 days/year for the first 5 years; 30 days/year for subsequent years).
- **Claim-Key Registry Binding:** The claim key `gratuity_entitlement_service_threshold` ("1 full year continuous service requirement for severance eligibility") must be bound strictly to **Article 51 Clause 2** (`FDL_33_2021_ART_51_02`), NOT Article 51 Clause 1. Binding foreign gratuity rules to Clause 1 constitutes an erroneous legal citation.

## 4. Technical Defect Analysis: Arabic PDF CMap Font Corruption

Official modification PDFs 1297 (FDL 20/2023) and 1760 (FDL 9/2024) embed subsetted fonts with corrupted ToUnicode CMap mapping tables.
- Standard extractors (`pdftotext`/PyMuPDF) emit presentation-form glyphs (`\ufe8e`) or replacement characters (`\ufffd`).
- **Architectural Remedy (`ADR-002`):** Phase 5.2 establishes **HTML-first deterministic extraction** from `uaelegislation.gov.ae`. PDF snapshots are preserved strictly as archival references (`source_role: "archival_reference"`). Any PDF-derived provision exhibiting CMap corruption is quarantined as `BLOCKED_LEGAL_TOPIC` until human extraction verification is recorded.
