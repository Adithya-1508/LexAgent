## 1. Executive Summary

This validation report provides an independent engineering and architectural audit of the proposed **Phase 5.2 Planning Package (GitHub Spec Kit)** for LexAgent (`https://github.com/Adithya-1508/LexAgent`).

Phase 5.2 expands LexAgent's statutory legal corpus from its Phase 5.1 MVP baseline (13 of 74 articles of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 and 1 of 39 articles of Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022) into a provenance-verified, bilingual, amendment-aware corpus with fail-closed retrieval, partial-release quarantine support, and version-scoped citation verification.

This report independently verifies:
1. **Source Code Baseline:** Every architectural assumption is reconciled against the actual codebase at `origin/main` (`ab14df9`).
2. **Schema Correctness:** All 7 canonical JSON Schemas in `specs/phase-5.2/contracts/` strictly comply with JSON Schema Draft 2020-12, including conditional schema constraints (`allOf`, `if`/`then`).
3. **Fixture Determinism:** All 14 positive and negative fixtures pass automated testing via `pytest` (61/61 passed in `tests/test_phase_5_2_schemas.py` with format checkers and conditional validation).
4. **Exact Test Classification:** All 145 baseline tests are cataloged individually into 4 tiers (117 Tier 1 Unchanged, 23 Tier 2 Opt-In, 5 Tier 3 Upgraded, 0 Failures) without approximate counts or double-counting (206 total tests across full suite, with 436 upstream warnings).
5. **Current-Law & Temporal Cutoff Governance:** Solves the missing-`as_of` contract by mandating that historical cutoffs (`effective_as_of_max = 2024-08-31`) are never silently presented as current law, adding mandatory `TEMPORAL_CUTOFF_DISCLOSURE`, 180-day freshness horizon fail-closed abstention for current-law queries, and fail-closed abstention for post-cutoff or incomplete amendments (`abstention_reason="stale_or_incomplete_amendment_coverage"`). A warning alone never authorizes a current-law answer.
6. **Requirements Traceability:** Full bidirectional traceability between functional requirements (`REQ-01`–`REQ-12`), tasks (`T-01`–`T-10`), and verification tests.
7. **CI Feasibility & Security:** Complete preservation of the `core-minimal` dependency isolation boundary, Bandit SAST security scans, and pip-audit vulnerability checks across all 11 internal GitHub Actions CI jobs. External third-party apps (such as CodeRabbit, which was rate-limited) are tracked and audited separately.

## 5. Architectural Decision Record (ADR) Feasibility Audit

1. **ADR-001 (Canonical JSON/JSONL + Release Manifest):** Feasible and verified. Eliminates SQLite cross-platform compile issues in CI while maintaining strict `pydantic` runtime independence.
2. **ADR-002 (HTML-First Extraction + 5-Dimension Provenance):** Feasible and verified. Direct response to the confirmed ToUnicode CMap corruption in modification PDFs 1297 and 1760.
3. **ADR-003 (Effective-Date Versioning with Closed Intervals):** Feasible and verified. Aligns with existing `models.py` and `retrieval/contracts.py` closed-interval implementations (`effective_from <= as_of <= effective_until`).
4. **ADR-004 (Bilingual Alignment with `unavailable_official` Support):** Feasible and verified. Prevents synthetic LLM text from polluting authoritative legal corpora while allowing complete Arabic coverage.
5. **ADR-005 (Fail-Closed Legacy Defaults):** Feasible and verified. Defaulting `publication_state` to `LEGACY_UNVERIFIED` ensures no unverified Phase 5.1 fixture is served in production without human/TLS verification.
6. **ADR-006 (Manual Acquisition Attestation):** Feasible and verified. Secures the manual drop-in boundary and blocks untrusted URLs from masquerading as verified government origin.
7. **ADR-007 (Decoupled Completeness & Partial Releases):** Feasible and verified. Solves the all-or-nothing release bottleneck and enables safe incremental ingestion.
